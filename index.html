About Folio

Folio is a minimalist college notes application built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. It focuses on clean typography, calm spacing, and simple organization without extra animations or clutter.

You can switch between Home, Notes, and About pages, save lecture notes directly in your browser, or download the source files to present in class.
